# UX4G & Indian Railways (GIGW 3.0) Design Rules

All frontend UI in this repository must adhere to UX4G (User Experience for Government) and GIGW 3.0 accessibility standards:

1. **Color & Contrast**:
   - Primary Navy Blue: `#003366` / `#0A2540`
   - Saffron Action: `#FF671F` / `#E65100`
   - India Green (Status Positive): `#046A38` / `#1B5E20`
   - Maintain minimum 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive controls.

2. **Accessibility (GIGW 3.0)**:
   - Visible keyboard focus rings (`focus:ring-2 focus:ring-[#FF671F]`).
   - Accessible ARIA labels on all icon-only buttons, modals, and timeline controls.
   - Scalable typography and screen-reader friendliness.

3. **Official e-Governance Hierarchy**:
   - Clear Indian Railways branding and ministry-grade header layouts.
   - Distinct badges for department ownership (TMS/Track Engineering, TDMS/TRD, SMMS/S&T).
